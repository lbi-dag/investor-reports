# Financial Analysis Checklist

Use the relevant sections; do not force every item into every report.

## Source Inventory

- Annual or interim financial statements
- Earnings/results release
- Investor presentation
- Regulatory filing
- Earnings-call transcript or prepared remarks
- Guidance update, strategy announcement, or material transaction release
- Prior-period release needed to test comparisons or changed framing

Record each source's title, publication date, covered period, URL or file path, and whether it is audited.

## Performance Baseline

- Revenue: reported, organic, constant currency, acquisitions, disposals
- Profitability: gross margin, EBITDA/EBIT, operating margin, net income
- Adjustments: restructuring, impairment, acquisition, litigation, stock compensation, other exclusions
- Cash: operating cash flow, free cash flow definition, working capital, capex
- Balance sheet: cash, gross debt, net debt, leverage, maturities, covenants
- Segments/geographies: growth, margins, mix, weak spots hidden by totals
- Per-share metrics: diluted share count, EPS, dividends, buybacks
- Guidance: metric definition, range, assumptions, changes from prior guidance

## Read-Between-the-Lines Tests

### Selective Comparisons

- Does management emphasize sequential improvement while year-over-year performance is weak?
- Does it highlight constant-currency growth while reported revenue declines?
- Does it change the comparison period or metric definition?
- Does it celebrate beating reduced guidance?

### Historical Consistency & Track Record

- **Dropped Metrics:** Has a previously highlighted KPI (e.g., "customer acquisition cost" or "net promoter score") disappeared from the current report?
- **Restatements:** Do current "prior year" comparables match the figures actually reported in the prior year's filing?
- **Narrative Shifts:** Has the strategic focus shifted (e.g., from "growth at all costs" to "efficiency") without acknowledging the pivot or the results of the prior strategy?
- **Guidance Reconciliation:** How does current performance compare to the specific targets set 6, 12, or 24 months ago?
- **"One-off" Persistence:** Are the same "one-time" restructuring charges appearing in multiple consecutive years?

### Adjusted Versus Reported

- How large is the adjustment gap?
- Are "one-time" costs recurring across periods?
- Do adjustments consume cash?
- Are adjusted metrics used consistently?

### Growth Quality

- Is growth organic, acquisition-driven, price-driven, or volume-driven?
- Does growth rely on a weak comparison period?
- Are customer count, units, traffic, retention, or backlog consistent with revenue claims?

### Cash And Balance Sheet

- Does profit convert into cash?
- Does free cash flow exclude recurring or necessary spending?
- Why did debt rise or fall?
- Are buybacks offsetting dilution or genuinely reducing shares?
- Are dividends and buybacks sensible given leverage and maturities?

### Guidance And Strategy

- Is guidance specific enough to test?
- What must improve for guidance to be achieved?
- Has the delivery date moved?
- Are restructuring programs evidence of decisive action, prior execution failure, or both?

### Language Patterns

Potential phrases to test, never decode automatically:

- "Challenging environment"
- "Temporary headwinds"
- "Strategic investment"
- "Efficiency actions"
- "Portfolio optimization"
- "Rationalization"
- "Rightsizing"
- "Strong underlying performance"
- "Significant improvement"
- "On track"
- "Material increase"

For each phrase, test whether disclosed figures support the framing and whether omitted context changes its meaning.

## Confidence

- **High:** directly supported by multiple disclosed figures or an explicit contradiction.
- **Medium:** supported by disclosed evidence but alternative explanations remain.
- **Low:** plausible interpretation with incomplete evidence; state what would confirm it.
