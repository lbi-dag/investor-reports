# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Investors, analysts, and financially literate readers use this project to
evaluate public hearing-care companies across the same reporting timeline.
They arrive with limited time and need to understand what changed, what
management emphasized, what management obscured, and which official sources
support each conclusion.

## Product Purpose

Investor Reports turns official company financial materials into concise,
evidence-based investor briefs. It reconciles reported and adjusted figures,
identifies selective comparisons and changing narratives, and makes
cross-company competitive dynamics easier to inspect.

Success means a reader can quickly identify the latest material developments,
judge the strength of management's narrative, open the supporting analysis, and
trace material claims back to official sources.

## Brand Personality

Analytical, skeptical, and authoritative. The product should feel calm,
precise, and independent rather than promotional or theatrical.

## Anti-references

- Promotional corporate investor-relations sites that prioritize brand imagery,
  management messaging, or vague growth claims over evidence.
- Generic SaaS dashboards built from interchangeable metric cards, excessive
  gradients, decorative icons, and inflated marketing copy.
- Trading terminals that create visual density without helping readers
  distinguish signal from noise.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the conclusion, then make the evidence easy to inspect.
2. Use hierarchy to distinguish signal, context, and supporting detail.
3. Make comparisons across companies and periods explicit and honest.
4. Prefer precise language and meaningful data over decoration.
5. Treat skepticism as a method, not a visual gimmick.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet a minimal practical accessibility baseline: readable contrast, semantic
HTML, visible keyboard focus, usable responsive layouts, descriptive links, and
reduced-motion handling for any nonessential animation.
