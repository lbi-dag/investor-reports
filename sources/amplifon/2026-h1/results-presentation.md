2026 Outlook
Entering Q3 with continued momentum
 During 2025, we implemented meaningful initiatives and invested substantially to accelerate future revenue
growth and structurally enhance profitability
 In H1 2026, these initiatives and investments delivered tangible results, driving improvements in both organic
growth and profitability
 Looking ahead to 2026, we confirm our expectations for:
 Global market demand growth in the region of +3%
1
 Continued outperformance in our key individual markets, driving market share gains, with organic growth above +3%
2
 A material increase in profitability in the region of +100 bps
 Prospective GN Hearing acquisition: integration planning is progressing well and is fully on track to ensure Day 1
readiness following the necessary regulatory approvals
 Looking beyond 2026, we are highly confident and excited about our strong prospects for profitable growth,
further strengthened by the transformational opportunity created by the GN Hearing acquisition
